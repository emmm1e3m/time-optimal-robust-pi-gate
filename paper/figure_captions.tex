\newcommand{\frontiercaption}{Exact speed--sensitivity frontier and its
weak-noise application.
(a) The minimum coefficient
$\chi=\lim_{f\to0}(1-F_{\mathrm{avg}})/f^2$, with $f=\delta/\Omega$,
for a full nominal $\pi$ gate at dimensionless duration $L=\Omega T$.
The shaded region is excluded for every measurable transverse control
satisfying $u_x^2+u_y^2\leq\Omega^2$.
The boundary is attained by the same-axis three-segment family~\cite{kukita2024}, joining
the ordinary $\pi$ pulse at $L=\pi$ to short-CORPSE~\cite{cummins2003}
at $L=7\pi/3$.
(b) Leading error budget divided by $s^2$ for zero-mean quasistatic detuning
and drive-independent weak Markovian relaxation and pure dephasing:
$\chi_{\min}(L)+\lambda L$, where
$s^2=\operatorname{Var}(\delta)/\Omega^2$,
$\lambda=\kappa/s^2$, and
$\kappa=(\Gamma_1+\Gamma_\phi)/(3\Omega)$.
The solid curve minimizes this budget over duration; the dashed and dotted
curves use the two endpoint pulses.
At $\lambda=1/(2\pi)$, the endpoints have equal budgets, while partial
compensation with $L_{\mathrm{opt}}\simeq2.08\pi$ lowers the leading budget
by $4.91\%$, the maximum relative reduction over $\lambda>0$
compared with the better endpoint pulse at this order.
(c) The corresponding optimal duration jumps at
$\lambda_c\simeq0.176$. The gray dashed lines mark $\lambda_c$ and $L_c/\pi$.
The two filled points at $\lambda_c$ represent
the simultaneous global minima $L=\pi$ and $L=L_c\simeq2.047\pi$;
no intermediate duration is optimal there.
The optimizations in (b) and (c) concern the leading weak-noise budget.}
